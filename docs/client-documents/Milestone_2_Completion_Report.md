# Milestone 2 Completion Report

**Prepared for:** Wayheld  
**Document type:** Client progress report  
**Milestone:** Milestone 2 — Core Journey Experience

---

## Overview

Milestone 2 brings Wayheld from a signed-in foundation into a living travel companion. Travellers can now begin a journey, shape how it should feel, discover places with intention, and receive a day-by-day itinerary written in Wayheld’s calm editorial voice. This milestone also delivers Explore, Wishlist, journey editing, past journeys, and a clearer home experience — so the product finally feels like planning with a knowledgeable friend, not filling out a form.

---

## What We Delivered

### Dashboard

When a traveller signs in, they arrive at a quiet home screen that asks a single question: *What would you like to do?*

From the dashboard, they can:

- **Begin a New Journey** — open the journey workspace and start shaping a destination.
- **Continue Journey** — return to a draft in progress, or reopen a journey that is ready to read.
- See a clear snapshot of their account: remaining AI credits, number of journeys, and places saved to the Wishlist.
- View a notifications area that shows whether anything new is waiting.

The tone matches the brand: warm greetings, unhurried copy, and a calm layout that never rushes the traveller into decisions.

### Journey Creation

Journey creation follows a considered, step-by-step path — closer to a conversation than a checklist.

Travellers can:

1. **Choose a destination** — search a country, region, or city with guided place suggestions, or select from popular destinations.
2. **Set the journey feel** — choose how the journey should feel, including Unhurried, Balanced, Active, Nature-focused, History-forward, Cultural, Urban, Rural, Photography-focused, Famous Landmarks, and Literary-rich.
3. **Shape the path** — optionally add a starting point, an ending point, and up to five must-visit places along the way.
4. **Choose when** — set a duration or a date range for the journey.
5. **Review** — confirm the shape of the journey before moving into discovery.

Progress is saved as they move, so a traveller can leave and return without losing their place. If credits are no longer available on a free plan, they are gently guided toward membership options rather than blocked without explanation.

### AI Journey Generation

Once the journey is shaped, Wayheld moves into Discovery and composition — the heart of the companion experience.

**During Discovery, travellers can:**

- Browse thoughtfully written place suggestions for their destination.
- Add or remove places on a personal journey board.
- Rename the journey.
- Save places they love to the Wishlist.
- Request more places when they want a wider view.
- Finish exploring when at least one place has been chosen.

**While the itinerary is being composed, travellers see:**

- A calm preparing screen with destination context and journey feel.
- A clear sense that the journey is being crafted — without noise, progress bars, or technical language.
- Helpful recovery options if something pauses, including trying again or returning to Discovery.

**When the journey is ready, travellers receive:**

- A day-by-day itinerary with themes, places, narrative guidance, and practical notes.
- Gentle pacing cues and thoughtful warnings where a day may be too full.
- An overview that feels editorial and human — not a list of tourist traps.

The AI does not speak like a chatbot. It behaves like a quiet companion who has been to these places and cares how the traveller experiences them.

### Explore

Explore lets travellers discover places independently of a full journey setup.

They can:

- Search a city, country, or route.
- Start from trending destinations or recent searches.
- Refine suggestions with intention-led filters such as cafés, restaurants, vegan-friendly options, local-feel places, hiking, museums, galleries, and quieter corners.
- Browse place cards with short, heritage-aligned descriptions.
- Add a place into an existing journey, or save it to the Wishlist for later.

Explore is designed for curiosity without pressure — browse first, decide later.

### Wishlist

The Wishlist holds places that felt meaningful, whether they came from Discovery or from exploring on their own.

Travellers can:

- View all saved places in one calm collection.
- Search and filter by destination or category.
- Sort by newest or oldest.
- Remove places they no longer need.
- Add a saved place into a journey when the timing feels right.

The Wishlist is private and personal — a quiet shelf of places waiting for the right journey, not a social feed.

### Journey Editing

A generated journey is not locked. Travellers can continue shaping it with care.

They can:

- Open any day and edit its places.
- Reorder stops, move places between days, lock important stops, or remove what no longer belongs.
- Add unused Discovery selections into the itinerary.
- Return to Discovery to find more places, then come back into editing.
- Ask Wayheld to regenerate a single day when the pacing or tone needs a fresh pass.
- Remove an entire day when the journey needs more breathing room (while keeping at least one day).

Editing stays structured and calm. Changes are intentional, not overwhelming. Remaining refinements and access windows are shown clearly according to the traveller’s plan.

### Past Journeys

Travellers can return to journeys they have already lived or marked complete.

They can:

- Open **Past Journeys** to see completed journeys in one place.
- Manage all journeys from the **Journeys** area — including drafts in progress and ready itineraries.
- Search and sort journeys.
- Rename, archive, mark completed or ready, or delete a journey when needed.
- Continue unfinished drafts from where they left off.

Returning to a past journey feels like revisiting a place — not digging through files.

### User Experience Improvements

Throughout Milestone 2, the experience was refined to feel quieter, clearer, and more Wayheld:

- Soft, personalised greetings on the dashboard for first visits and returning travellers.
- Auto-saving during journey creation so progress is never lost.
- Editorial step language that guides without rushing (“Begin,” “Explore,” “Feeling called”).
- Loading states and empty states written in the brand voice rather than generic system messages.
- Gentle confirmations when saving places, updating a profile, or changing a journey.
- Clear credit visibility so travellers always know what remains.
- Profile updates for personal details, location search, and security.
- Onboarding that remembers travel style, interests, pace, and preferences across sessions.
- Mobile-friendly navigation with a calm sidebar for the full journey library.

### Stability Improvements

Milestone 2 also focused on making the product feel dependable in everyday use:

- Safer recovery when Discovery or composition pauses, with clear options to try again.
- Protection against losing draft progress during journey setup.
- Clear messaging when credits are exhausted, instead of silent failure.
- Consistent handling of journey actions such as rename, archive, complete, and delete.
- Smoother loading across key screens so the experience feels composed rather than abrupt.
- Stronger overall readiness for continued use as travellers create, revisit, and refine real journeys.

## Notes

Several refinements were completed during this milestone beyond the core feature set. The journey flow was shaped to feel like companionship rather than configuration. Discovery was placed before final composition so travellers choose places with intention. Editing was designed around days and places rather than overwhelming options. Language throughout the product was kept quiet, grounded, and observational — consistent with the original Wayheld voice guide.

The visual system was also brought into a warmer, light editorial presentation that supports the slow-travel feeling of the brand.

## Summary

Milestone 2 delivers the core of Wayheld: a traveller can begin a journey, discover places with care, receive a thoughtful day-by-day itinerary, refine it, save what matters, and return to past journeys — all in a calm, private experience. The product now carries the philosophy described in the original Wayheld overview: pace over pressure, presence over noise, and guidance that feels human.

Wayheld is no longer only a beautiful foundation. It is a working companion for meaningful travel.

## Typography

| Element | Choice |
| --- | --- |
| **Heading Font** | **Fraunces** — a soft editorial serif with quiet character |
| **Body Font** | **Inter** — clean, readable sans for interface and body copy |

### Font Sizes (commonly used)

| Role | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Micro labels | `0.65rem` – `0.7rem` |
| Small / captions | `0.75rem` (`text-xs`) |
| Body | `0.875rem` – `1rem` (`text-sm` / `text-base`) |
| Supporting lead | `1.125rem` (`text-lg`) |
| Card / section titles | `1.25rem` – `1.875rem` (`text-xl` – `text-3xl`) |
| Section headlines | Fluid scale roughly `2.25rem` to `4rem` |
| Marketing hero headlines | Fluid scale roughly `2.75rem` to `5.5rem` |

Headings typically use light weight, tight tracking, and balanced line height for an editorial feel.

## Shape & Depth

| Token | Value | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Border Radius — Buttons / Chips** | Fully rounded (`pill`) | Primary and secondary buttons, chips, badges |
| **Border Radius — Inputs / Options** | `1rem` (`rounded-2xl`) | Forms, selectable option cards |
| **Border Radius — Cards** | `1.5rem` (`rounded-3xl`) | Standard cards and panels |
| **Border Radius — Featured Stage** | `2rem` (`rounded-4xl`) | Larger featured surfaces |
| **Shadow — Panel** | Soft, deep, low-contrast charcoal shadow | Elevated panels |
| **Shadow — Card** | Slightly stronger soft shadow | Journey and content cards |
| **Shadow — Stage / Featured** | Tall soft shadow, sometimes with a moss tint | Featured or highlighted surfaces |

## Buttons

| Style | Appearance |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary** | Pill-shaped, moss green fill (`#74876B`), light text on cream, gentle lift on hover |
| **Secondary** | Pill-shaped, transparent with warm border, charcoal text, soft hover fill |
| **Ghost** | Text-forward link style with moss accent on hover |

Buttons feel quiet and assured — never loud, never urgent.

## Cards

Cards are white surfaces on the warm parchment background, with soft borders, generous padding, and understated shadows. They read as calm editorial panels rather than busy dashboard tiles. Illustrations may fade gently into the edge of a card for atmosphere without clutter.

## Overall Design Style

**Luxury editorial / calm heritage travel.**

The look is warm, light, and unhurried: parchment backgrounds, charcoal typography, moss accents, soft serif headlines, and generous space. It avoids neon gradients, hard contrast, and “tech product” flash. The feeling is closer to a well-made travel journal than a booking engine.

## Brand Summary

Wayheld looks and feels like a quiet companion for slow travel. The visual identity is warm parchment, deep charcoal, and moss green — soft enough to feel restorative, clear enough to feel trustworthy. Fraunces gives headlines a literary, human quality; Inter keeps the interface readable and composed. Everything is designed to lower the pulse: rounded edges, gentle shadows, restrained colour, and room to breathe. The brand does not shout. It invites the traveller to linger.
